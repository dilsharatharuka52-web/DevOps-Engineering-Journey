Social coding solution
. Discuss with the repo owner
. Agree to develop it
. Open an issue and assign it to yourself
. Fork the code and make your changes

Pair Programming
. Tow programmers on one workstation
. The driver is typing
. The navigator is reviewing
. Every 20 minutes they switch

Pair programming benefits
. Higher code quality
. Defects fount earlier
. Lower maintenance costs
. Skills transfer
. Tow set of eyes on every line of code
. Broader understanding of the codebase
